Data Deletion Instructions

Ethos School of Tennis · Last updated 9 July 2026

You have the right to have your personal data deleted. To request deletion of any data we hold about you, including data received when you message us on Instagram or another platform:

  1. Email privacy@ethosschooloftennis.com with the subject line "Delete my data", or send us a direct message on the platform you contacted us through.
  2. Tell us the platform and the handle/name you used so we can locate your records.
  3. We will confirm and permanently delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records.

Deleting your data means we remove your contact details, messaging identifiers, and message history from our systems.

Contact

Email: privacy@ethosschooloftennis.com
