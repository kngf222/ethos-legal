Privacy Policy

Ethos School of Tennis · Last updated 9 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ethos School of Tennis ("Ethos", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our services, including our website, our booking and coaching tools, and our messaging on platforms such as Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Discord.

Information We Collect

  • Contact & profile data you provide: name, email, phone number, and social handle.
  • Messaging data when you message us on Instagram or another platform: your platform-scoped user ID, message content, and timestamps, received through the official Meta (Instagram) Graph API and equivalent platform APIs.
  • Booking & payment data needed to schedule and fulfill lessons and memberships (payment is processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers).
  • Usage data such as pages viewed and actions taken, used to improve the service.

How We Use Information

  • To reply to your messages and provide customer support within platform messaging windows.
  • To schedule, confirm, and deliver coaching sessions and memberships.
  • To send you information you request and service updates.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our services.

Instagram & Meta Platform Data

When you send us a direct message on Instagram, Meta provides us your Instagram-scoped ID and message so we can respond. We use this data only to communicate with you and to provide the service you contacted us about. We do not sell it. We do not use it for unrelated advertising. Our use of information received from Meta APIs follows the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate (for example, hosting, scheduling, and payment processing), and only as needed to provide the service, or where required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. See our Data Deletion instructions or contact us below.
